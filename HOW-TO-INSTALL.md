# Put Taal online (free, about 2 minutes)

1. Unzip this folder on a computer.
2. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop and sign up free (Google login works).
3. Drag the whole "taal-app" folder onto the page.
4. Netlify gives you a link like https://something.netlify.app. Rename it under Site configuration, e.g. taal-riya.netlify.app.

To update the app later, drag the new folder onto the same site's "Deploys" page.

# Install on the phone

- Android (Chrome): open the link, tap the three-dot menu, then "Install app".
- iPhone (Safari): open the link, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".

Taal then opens full screen from its own icon, works without internet, and its WhatsApp buttons open WhatsApp directly.

# Keep data safe

Data is saved on the phone. In Settings, tap "Save backup file" now and then and keep the file in Google Drive or WhatsApp "Message yourself".
To move to a new phone: install Taal there, open Settings, and tap "Restore from file".
